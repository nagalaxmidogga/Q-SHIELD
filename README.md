# Q-SHIELD ⚛️

## Quantum AI-Based Drug-Abuse Risk Mapping & Prevention

### 🚨 Problem

Substance abuse is a serious social and public-health challenge. 
Prevention teams have limited resources and may not know which areas 
should receive priority support first.

Q-SHIELD addresses this challenge using Artificial Intelligence 
and Quantum Computing to support data-driven prevention planning.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Q-SHIELD is a privacy-aware system that:

- Estimates substance-abuse risk at an area level
- Identifies potential high-risk areas
- Uses Quantum Optimization to prioritize intervention areas
- Helps organizations allocate limited prevention resources
- Supports awareness, counselling, healthcare outreach and rehabilitation planning

The system focuses on **prevention and community support**, not on 
tracking individuals or locating illegal substances.

---

## ⚛️ Why Quantum Computing?

Selecting the best areas for intervention while working with limited 
resources is a **constrained optimization problem**.

We use the **Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA)** 
to explore possible combinations of priority areas.

Each area is represented by a binary decision:

- `1` → Select the area for intervention
- `0` → Do not select the area

The quantum optimizer considers the risk score and intervention 
budget to produce a priority selection.
